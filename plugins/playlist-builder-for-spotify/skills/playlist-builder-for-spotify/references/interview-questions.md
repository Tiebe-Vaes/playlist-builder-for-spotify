# Interview questions

One round, asked together. Each question offers 3 or 4 options; the user can always type their own answer. Skip any question the request already answered ("a 2 hour study playlist" answers occasion and length).

## Occasion

What is the playlist for?

- Studying or focus
- Commute or road trip
- Winding down or sleep
- Workout or party

## Mood and energy

What should it feel like?

- Calm and mellow
- Sad or nostalgic
- Upbeat and warm
- High energy

## Length

How long should it run?

- About 30 minutes
- About 1 hour
- About 2 hours
- 3 hours or more

Write the answer down in minutes for the generate step.

## Familiar vs discovery

How much should be new to you?

- Mostly songs I already know
- A mix of known and new
- Mostly new artists

## Follow-ups

Ask a follow-up only when an answer leaves the description ambiguous:

- **Party or workout**: "Mostly vocals, or fine with instrumentals and remixes?"
- **Sleep or focus**: "Lyrics OK, or mostly instrumental?"
- **Shared listening** (a party, a car with friends): "Should it lean towards songs everyone knows?"

Anything the user says here goes into the description word for word. Anything they don't say stays out.
