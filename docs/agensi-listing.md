## Listing description

Build Spotify playlists you actually want to hear. Instead of guessing from one sentence, Claude asks a few short questions, lets you judge real sample tracks, picks seed playlists from your own library, and then writes one precise prompt for Spotify's playlist generator.

What it does:

- Builds a playlist for an occasion or mood ("songs for studying", "a road trip mix").
- Starts from one song you can't stop playing, artists you name, or what you've been listening to lately.
- Finds a few new songs like ones you love, without making a playlist.
- Merges your playlists, with rules like "drop the songs I'm sick of".
- Remembers your defaults in an optional taste profile, so repeat runs take one line.

Requirements:

- Claude (claude.ai, Claude Desktop or Claude Code) with the Spotify connector connected. The skill uses Claude's Spotify connector and does not work in other agents.
- Spotify Premium to generate playlists. Free accounts can still use songs mode.

The skill needs no API keys, runs no scripts and makes no network calls of its own: every Spotify call goes through the connector you approve in Claude.
